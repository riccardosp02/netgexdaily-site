personal project SPX / NDX market data

The market's net gamma exposure, read one day at a time.

Every day I collect, compute and publish the net GEX for SPX and NDX from the options chains, then visualize it in interactive dashboards to read where the market tends to accelerate or settle down.

dashboards

Two indices, same methodology

Each dashboard lets you pick a day from a calendar and shows three linked charts: spot price with the levels where gamma exposure is highest and lowest, the CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta), and the net gamma exposure value, each sampled at 5-minute intervals.

methodology

From the options chain to the chart

The site doesn't run a traditional database: it uses Google Sheets as lightweight, inspectable storage, one published sheet per day, indexed by a master file the site reads automatically.

01Data source — SPX / NDX options chain
02Collection script — computes net GEX and saves a row
03Google Sheets — one sheet per day of data
04CSV publishing — day sheet + master index
05Dashboard — fetches the CSV and renders the charts
